Research date: all pages fetched 7 Oct 2026 unless noted. Method note: the session's web-search budget ran out partway through, so several products (Kin, Bond, Hippo, Fabriq, People Notes) could not be verified. They are listed under Gaps. Many roundup articles are written by vendors (Dex, Orvo, Dextr, YourPond, BlaBlaNote, Wave Connect, Contacts+). ## 1. Which products are alive, acquired, pivoted or shut down in 2026?

### Takeaway
The first wave of manual "keep in touch" apps has mostly died or gone stale (UpHabit closed on 9 Apr 2026; Garden has not been updated since June 2020; Mogul since Jan 2024; Fabriq's domain no longer resolves). A newer wave of small, 2025-2026 apps built around AI and voice (BlaBlaNote, Nametrace, YourPond, Dextr, Connections, Orvo) now covers parts of the "speak a note about someone you just met" job. None of them combines voice capture, GPS/where-met, a map with a city filter, and WhatsApp intake in one product.

### Inferences
- Churn in this category is high. Most single-founder "reminder" apps (Garden, Mogul, Fabriq, UpHabit's consumer app) stalled or died. Revenue was hard to sustain at $0–$10/month, and the survivors either moved to B2B (Cloze toward real estate, UpHabit toward Salesforce) or added AI.
- The 2025-2026 entrants share one pattern: they capture by voice or natural language, have an AI layer, and make a privacy promise. That confirms the new app is aimed at a live trend. It also means "voice note → structured contact" is no longer unique by itself.

## 2. Deep dives: the 6-7 products most relevant to a mobile-first, voice-first "people memory" app

### Takeaway
The closest competitors to the new app are **YourPond**, **BlaBlaNote**, **Nametrace** and **Dextr**.
- **YourPond:** voice "Describe" entry, contact photos, a map clustered by city, and "see who's there" before trips. It is iOS-only, consumer and friendship oriented, with no business-card scanning and no WhatsApp intake.
- **BlaBlaNote:** voice notes with transcription and extraction, WhatsApp and Telegram intake, a QR card, an event mode, and AI over your network via MCP. It has no map or geotag features.
- **Nametrace:** voice notes, place and date capture, business-card scanning, natural-language search, vCard export, one-click account deletion, and Swiss hosting.
- **Dextr:** a local-first iOS app with "where and when you met", card scanning, and a very low price of $14.99/yr.

**Contacts+** and **Cloze** are mature but not voice-first. Contacts+ is the sync, enrichment and card-scan utility; Cloze is a passive-capture CRM that has drifted toward real estate.

### Inferences
- **YourPond is the nearest consumer-side analogue** to the planned list, profile and map with a city picker, plus voice capture and photos. It is personal and friendship oriented, iOS-only (web for typing), has no business-card OCR and no WhatsApp intake, and its free tier is capped at 25 contacts. That cap is unsuitable for someone meeting hundreds of people.
- **BlaBlaNote is the nearest business-side analogue** for voice, WhatsApp intake and an AI assistant over your contacts (MCP). It has no geotag or map, and its pricing is in EUR. Its free tier is limited by recording minutes (90), not by contacts.
- **Nametrace is the nearest on "place and date of meeting" plus business-card scanning, vCard export and one-click deletion**, with a strong Swiss privacy story. Its UI is in English and German only, and its value comes from enrichment credits rather than capture speed.
- **No product found combines all of the following:** a one-tap voice capture, automatic GPS stamp, a map with clustered pins and a city picker, photos of the person, card and place, plus WhatsApp voice-note intake. The combination of a multi-country traveller's map and where-met GPS is the clearest gap.
- Several apps have converged on MCP (BlaBlaNote, Contacts+) as their "AI assistant over your contacts" approach. This suggests the planned AI assistant is becoming table stakes.

### Gaps
- No App Store or Google Play rating or review data was obtained for BlaBlaNote, Cloze, Contacts+ or Queue (app IDs unknown, and the search budget was exhausted).
- Offline capture behaviour is undocumented for every deep-dive product except Dextr (inferred) and Wave Connect Enterprise (documented).
- Whether Nametrace auto-stamps GPS, or whether "place" is a manual field, could not be confirmed. The App Store privacy label lists precise location, which suggests GPS use.
- Cloze's business-card scanner and data export status were not verified on official pages in 2026.

## 3. Briefer profiles: other notable products (status, what they do, pricing)

### Takeaway
The rest of the market splits into five groups:
1. Email- or Gmail-centric relationship trackers (Nat, Orvo, Regards).

## 4. Notion and Airtable personal CRM templates

### Takeaway
Notion's marketplace lists about 250 personal CRM templates. Most are free; paid ones are typically single-digit dollars (e.g. Easlo's CRM at $9). They offer birthday fields, last-contacted dates, tags and relationship tiers. They fail at the moment that matters for the new product: quick capture on a phone, reliable reminders, and syncing with the phone's contacts.

### Cited Findings
- Notion's marketplace "Personal CRM" category shows about 250 templates. Visible listings were labelled "Free". Creators include EdriansNotes, The Blueprint, Hey Gloria, GizmoThemes and Notion itself. The category pitch: "Never forget a birthday or the last conversation you had again." — [Notion templates: Personal CRM](https://www.notion.com/templates/category/personal-crm)
- 2sync's 4 Oct 2026 roundup:
  - Personal-oriented templates: "Circles of Trust" (free; birthday tracking, relationship tiers) and "Contact List (Personal CRM)" (free; last-contacted dates, tags).
  - Paid example: Easlo's CRM at **$9** on Gumroad.
  - Stated limitation: Notion works as a CRM only if "your contact count stays under a few thousand, you sync Notion with the place where your contacts already exist (phone, email), and you commit to actually opening Notion during the work day."
  - — [2sync: best Notion CRM templates](https://2sync.com/blog/best-notion-crm-templates)
- **Mobile limitations:** Notion's mobile app "lacks specialized mobile CRM features"; you cannot easily call from a record. The weak spot is "everything after you close the browser tab", e.g. a call that arrives without a saved name, or a reminder that "stayed in Notion". — [Lark: Notion CRM](https://www.larksuite.com/en_us/blog/notion-crm); [zeeg: Notion CRM guide](https://zeeg.me/en/blog/post/notion-crm)

### Inferences

## 5. User complaints, praise, and privacy stances (cross-product)

### Takeaway
Across the category, the praise is for simplicity and quick logging (Garden, Mogul, YourPond's voice entry). The complaints are about missing integrations (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, calendar), clutter or complexity (Cloze), and reliability (Cloze resets). Privacy has become a selling point: almost every 2025-2026 entrant promises "no selling, no ads, no AI training", and Dextr and Nametrace go further with local-only storage and Swiss hosting respectively.

### Cited Findings
- **Garden:** praised for simplicity and for replacing a $20/month subscription. Criticised for lacking email, Google Voice, Instagram and WhatsApp integration. — [App Store: Garden](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1230466454)
- **Mogul:** praised for a clean interface and simplicity ("don't know what I would do without it"). Users request LinkedIn integration, calendar syncing, and multiple people per interaction. — [App Store: Mogul](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1367307210)
- **YourPond:** voice entry praised for quickly logging names instead of keeping scattered notes. — [App Store: YourPond](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761669539)
- **Cloze (Capterra, 4.4/5, 16 reviews):** praised for passive auto-capture and the mobile app. Complaints: resets every 3–4 months, slow support, cluttered dashboard, too technical. — [Capterra: Cloze](https://www.capterra.com/p/186061/Cloze/)
- **Connections:** praised as handy when "someone AirDrops a contact, and you forgot their name" (only 1 rating). — [App Store: Connections](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6575368930)
- **Privacy stances, by product:**
  - **Nametrace:** Swiss hosting, FADP/GDPR, 2FA by default, no data selling, one-click deletion. — [nametrace.app](https://nametrace.app/en)
  - **Dextr:** data stays on device and in iCloud, "never sent to us". — [Dextr pricing](https://dextr.app/pricing/)
  - **YourPond:** "We don't sell your data, use it for ads, or train AI on it". — [App Store: YourPond](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6761669539)
  - **BlaBlaNote:** encryption at rest and in transit, GDPR, the AI provider deletes data after processing and does not train on it. — [blablanote.com](https://blablanote.com/)
  - **Queue:** "never sell it or show you an ad". — [queue.community](https://queue.community/)
- **App Store privacy labels:**
  - Garden: links contact info and contacts list to identity. — [App Store: Garden](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1230466454)
  - Connections: collects coarse location and identifiers. — [App Store: Connections](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6575368930)
  - Nametrace: links precise location and audio. — [App Store: Nametrace](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nametrace/id6758048527)

### Inferences
- For an app that stores voice recordings, GPS and photos of people, the market expects at minimum: a "no selling, no ads, no AI training" pledge, data export, and one-click account deletion. The privacy-forward options (on-device storage like Dextr, or regional hosting like Nametrace) set the bar. This matters especially for UAE, Australia and Thailand users, where data-residency questions may come up.
- The recurring "missing WhatsApp" complaint (Garden), together with BlaBlaNote's and Cloze's WhatsApp support, suggests WhatsApp voice-note intake is a real differentiator for markets where WhatsApp dominates (UAE, Australia business use). LINE is likely more relevant in Thailand (not researched).

### Gaps
- No G2 data, and no App Store or Play reviews, for BlaBlaNote, Nametrace (too few ratings), Contacts+ or Queue.
- No Reddit sentiment (fetch blocked; search budget exhausted).
- Product Hunt comments for 2024-2026 personal-CRM launches were not collected.
