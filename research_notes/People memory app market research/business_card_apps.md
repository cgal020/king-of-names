# Business card scanning and digital business card apps (status as of October 2026)

All pages were fetched or searched on 2026-10-07 unless a different date is shown. Method caveat: most pages were read through an automated fetch-and-summarise tool, so the figures are that tool's reading of each page. Figures that look unusual are marked "verify". The web-search budget for this session ran out partway through, so a few items were verified by direct page fetch only, and some remain open (see the Gaps sections).

## 1. Status in 2026: alive, acquired, pivoted or shut down, and who owns each product

### Takeaway
Nearly all the major products are alive and actively updated. Several have changed shape. CamCard (INTSIG) has rebranded around an "AI Business Assistant" with voice transcription. Linq pivoted its company focus to an iMessage/RCS AI-messaging API, though the card app remains. ABBYY Business Card Reader is end-of-life on Android, and on iOS it has lost sync. ScanBizCards is still an independent US company; I found no evidence that Covve acquired it. The category is moving from "share my card" toward AI event lead capture: badge scanning, enrichment, AI notes and AI follow-ups.

## 2. What each product does, its platforms, and whether it is mainly for sharing your own card or capturing other people

### Takeaway
Most products are mainly "share my card" tools (QR, NFC, wallet, email signature) with a scanner added on. The ones that lean toward capturing other people are CamCard, Covve Scan, ScanBizCards, Zoho Card Scanner and Eight, plus the new AI lead-capture apps (NeverDrop, AI CardVault, Leadnics, CardDrop). Blinq and Popl now sit in both camps through AI notes and event capture. None of them is built around a quiet, personal memory of people.

### Cited Findings
- **CamCard** comes in two editions. CamCard Business is for capture: unlimited scanning, AI recognition, CRM sync, meeting/visit/call notes, task assignment and AI email writing. CamCard Digital Business Card is for sharing — [CamCard pricing](https://www.camcard.com/pricing). The consumer app adds real-time voice-to-text with AI summaries and audio-file transcription (a 1-hour file in about 5 minutes) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id349447615). It runs on iOS and Android.
- **HiHello** is mainly a digital card tool (QR, NFC, Wallet, AirDrop, widgets, email signatures). It has an AI scanner with bulk "stack" scanning, plus notes, tags and analytics. It runs on iOS, Android and web — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hihello-digital-business-card/id1378114205)
- **Blinq** is a digital card (20+ fields, QR/NFC/Wallet/Apple Watch/widget). It adds a paper card scanner, the "AI Notetaker" and event lead capture. It runs on iOS, Android, Apple Watch and Wear OS — [Blinq mobile app](https://blinq.me/solutions/mobile-app); [AI Notetaker](https://blinq.me/solutions/ai-notetaker)
- **Popl** focuses on event lead capture for teams: a universal badge scanner, enrichment, voice recording for in-person meetings, AI notes and AI follow-up emails. It also sells digital cards and NFC hardware — [popl.co](https://popl.co/)
- **Covve Scan** is a capture tool for cards, badges, LinkedIn QRs and digital profiles. It adds digital and NFC cards and a scanning API (OCR engine) — [covve.com](https://covve.com/)
- **ScanBizCards** captures cards and badges with OCR plus a human transcription option, and runs on iOS and Android — [scanbizcards.com](https://www.scanbizcards.com/)
- **Wave Connect** is a digital card with an AI paper card scanner, AI badge scanner, auto-enrichment, notes, tags, reminders, automated follow-ups and CRM sync — [wavecnct.com](https://wavecnct.com/)
- **Tapt** (AU) sells NFC/QR cards with "no app required" for the other person. It can scan cards, QR codes and badges, enrich contacts with AI, and push to HubSpot/Salesforce — [tapt.io](https://tapt.io/)
- **Haystack** (AU) offers digital cards with an AI card scanner (unlimited on all tiers) — [Haystack pricing](https://www.thehaystackapp.com/pricing)
- **V1CE** (UK) pairs an NFC card with "Client Capture OS": two-way exchange, event tagging, the "Scout" AI networking assistant, AI enrichment, automated follow-up sequences, booking, contracts and payments — [V1CE pricing](https://v1ce.co/pages/pricing)
- **Mobilo** sells NFC cards plus an app with lead capture, a card scanner and CRM/Zapier on all plans — [Mobilo pricing](https://www.mobilocard.com/pricing)
- **Eight** exchanges cards by QR/NFC, digitises cards from photos, and notifies you of contacts' job changes and promotions. It runs on iOS, Android and desktop — [8card.net](https://8card.net/)
- **Zoho Card Scanner** is a free scanner that saves to Zoho CRM, Salesforce and Google, and includes AR lookup of existing CRM leads — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/card-scanner/id588799392)
- **HubSpot Business Card Scanner** is free and available only to HubSpot CRM users — [Spreadly blog](https://spreadly.app/en/blog/top-7-best-business-card-scanner-apps-2026)
- **New AI capture apps**:
  - **NeverDrop**: card OCR plus voice or text context, then an AI-drafted follow-up email "in under two minutes"; works with HubSpot and Gmail — [aggregator listing](https://dolphinvoice.ai/zh/ai-apps/never-drop-1080760). This is an aggregator source, so verify.
  - **Leadnics**: scans cards, badges and QR codes; voice-activated notes and tasks — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6739613530)
  - **AI CardVault**: AI OCR for multi-language cards, voice notes, Hot/Warm/Cold tags, Salesforce/HubSpot/Zoho — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ai-cardvault/id6740126760)
  - **ExpoWizard**: unlimited AI card scans — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/expowizard/id6742470029)
  - **CardDrop**: you text a photo of a card to the service, and it syncs the contact to Google Contacts. It has natural-language search, event tagging, follow-up reminders and notes, and deletes card images after 90 days. It is aimed at real-estate agents and brokers — [Capterra, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.capterra.com/p/10047916/carddrop/)
  - **Spreadly** (Munich): AI scanning of cards, badges, QR codes and handwritten notes — [Spreadly blog](https://spreadly.app/en/blog/top-7-best-business-card-scanner-apps-2026)

### Inferences
- The closest competitor to the target app's core loop (speak a note → transcript → linked to a contact) is **Blinq's AI Notetaker**, followed by **CamCard's voice transcription**, **Popl's voice recording** and **Covve's voice capture**. All four sit inside sales or lead-capture products. None pairs the voice note with GPS, a map or a city picker as the main way to browse people.
- CardDrop's "text a photo to a number" intake is the nearest thing to the planned WhatsApp intake.

### Gaps
- I did not obtain platform details (web app or PWA) for every vendor. None of them presented itself as a PWA.

## 3. Capture: card scanning and OCR accuracy, languages (especially Arabic and Thai), batch scanning, paper vs QR/NFC, and speed

### Takeaway
Accuracy claims run from 96% (Covve) to "99.99%" (CamCard). HiHello, ScanBizCards and Sansan/Eight use humans as a backstop. **Arabic** is confirmed only for CamCard among the major apps, and possibly in some generic Arabic-localised scanners. **Thai** is confirmed for CamCard and Covve. I found no product that publishes a tap count or time-to-save figure beyond marketing phrases like "in seconds" or "under two minutes".

### Cited Findings
- **CamCard**:
  - Claims "99.99% accurate AI recognition" and lists Arabic and Thai among its supported languages — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id349447615)
  - A third party describes 17 languages, up to 3 languages per card, and batch photo scanning — [Spreadly blog](https://spreadly.app/en/blog/top-7-best-business-card-scanner-apps-2026)
  - Premium includes "manual verification" — [App Store (VC) via search summary](https://apps.apple.com/vc/app/camcard-ai-scanner-transcribe/id349447615)
  - CamCard Business advertises "AI-Powered Card Recognition with Auto-Correction" and duplicate detection — [pricing](https://www.camcard.com/pricing)
  - A user review reports AI misreads (e.g. "MARIA" read as "MARUi") — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id349447615)
- **Covve Scan**:
  - Claims "96% accuracy" and calls itself the "most accurate" scanner. It supports "60+ languages and regional formats", handles multi-language cards, is "optimized for rapid, consecutive scans", and works online and offline — [Covve scanner page](https://covve.com/business-card-scanner)
  - The App Store language list includes **Thai** but not Arabic — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1459654107)
  - A Capterra list of Covve Scan languages also includes Thai, Indonesian, Malay and Vietnamese but **not Arabic** — [Capterra AE](https://www.capterra.ae/software/1011109/covve-scan)
  - A Capterra reviewer asked to "scan more than one card at a time" — [Software Advice AU](https://www.softwareadvice.com.au/software/357774/covve-scan)
- **HiHello**:
  - "Relies on a human workforce for providing the most accurate transcription" (CardMunch 2.0) — [HiHello blog](https://www.hihello.com/blog/hihello-cardmunch)
  - Built on multiple AI models, with human verification "if needed" — [search summary of hihello.com](https://www.hihello.com/features/business-card-scanner)
  - Supports bulk stack scanning with edge detection and auto-capture — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hihello-digital-business-card/id1378114205)
  - Scans paper cards, badges, QR codes, screens, signage and other card apps — [HiHello scanner page](https://www.hihello.com/features/business-card-scanner)
  - Scanner languages are not published.
- **ScanBizCards**: OCR plus a "Human Transcription service" with 100 free credits per user per year, 19 languages, and batch scanning — [scanbizcards.com](https://www.scanbizcards.com/). Manual transcription costs about $0.18 per card according to a third party — [Spreadly blog](https://spreadly.app/en/blog/top-7-best-business-card-scanner-apps-2026)
- **Sansan/Eight**: Sansan says it achieves 99.9% accuracy by combining AI data entry with a final human check, using "several thousand operators both in Japan and overseas" — [Sansan IR](https://ir.corp-sansan.com/en/ir/management/businessinformation_3.html). Eight's own page only claims "fast and accurate" digitisation and does not say whether humans are involved — [8card.net](https://8card.net/)
- **Wave Connect**: The AI scanner needs only first name, last name and company, then enriches phone, email, title and LinkedIn. It can scan old photos of cards. Paper card scans are free; QR and badge scans use enrichment credits (1 or 4) — [Wave docs (AR)](https://wavecnct.com/docs/ar/contacts/scanner.md)
- **Blinq**: Has a "universal contact scanner" on Premium and above — [Blinq pricing](https://blinq.me/pricing). Scanner languages are not published. One roundup says Blinq "verifies each scanned contact" — [Blinq's own blog](https://blinq.me/blog/best-business-card-scanner-apps). This is a vendor source.
- **Zoho Card Scanner**: 14 scanning languages (English, French, Spanish, German, Dutch, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish, Chinese, Russian and others). Arabic is not listed — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/card-scanner/id588799392)
- **ABBYY BCR** (legacy): 25 languages according to a 2026 third-party article, which also says "development reportedly slowed". Likely stale — [Spreadly blog](https://spreadly.app/en/blog/top-7-best-business-card-scanner-apps-2026)
- **Arabic-localised generic scanners** exist on the App Store. For example, "ماسح بطاقات العمل - ذكي" says it scans in all languages and lets you add notes, groups, **locations** and AI summaries to contacts — [App Store (ar)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%EC%8A%A4%EB%A7%88%ED%8A%B8-%EB%AA%85%ED%95%A8-%EB%A6%AC%EB%8D%94%EA%B8%B0/id6752925652?l=ar)
- **Speed claims**: Blinq says you can build a card "in less than two minutes" — [search summary](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1324102258). NeverDrop claims capture to AI follow-up in "under two minutes" — [aggregator](https://dolphinvoice.ai/zh/ai-apps/never-drop-1080760). Tapt says "Scan business cards, QR codes, and event badges in seconds" — [tapt.io](https://tapt.io/)
- **QR/NFC exchange vs paper**: Blinq, HiHello, Popl, Wave, Tapt, V1CE, Mobilo, Haystack and Eight all centre on QR/NFC exchange, where the other person needs no app — [Tapt](https://tapt.io/); [Blinq](https://blinq.me/solutions/mobile-app); [8card.net](https://8card.net/)

### Inferences
- For UAE and Thai use, **CamCard** is the only major incumbent with confirmed Arabic and Thai card OCR. An LLM-vision approach to OCR in the target app could beat incumbents on Arabic, or bilingual Arabic/English, cards. That needs testing.
- Human-in-the-loop transcription (HiHello, ScanBizCards, Sansan) remains the accuracy benchmark. It raises privacy questions, covered in section 6.

### Gaps
- I found no independent, current accuracy benchmark, and no published tap count or seconds-to-save figure for any app.
- Arabic and Thai support is unpublished for Blinq, HiHello, Popl, Wave, Tapt, Haystack and V1CE.

## 4. Context capture: where and when you met (location, event tags, map), voice notes, photos of the person, AI notes and AI follow-ups

### Takeaway
Voice and AI notes are now common in leading products: Blinq's AI Notetaker, CamCard's transcription, Popl's voice recording, Covve's voice capture, and the voice notes in NeverDrop, AI CardVault and Leadnics. **Event tagging** is common too. I found **no product that offers a map of where you met people, or a city filter**, as a confirmed feature. Location is at most collected in the background (privacy labels for Blinq and CamCard) or used for event navigation (Popl). I found no evidence of capturing a photo of the person, as opposed to the card.

### Cited Findings
- **Blinq AI Notetaker**:
  - You can record a conversation on phone or watch, "or capture a voice note afterward", and it can also transcribe phone calls. It produces a transcript, a summary and next steps, all auto-linked to a contact. It supports 99+ languages with auto-detection, notes of up to 8 hours each, unlimited notes, speaker identification and Bluetooth mics, and it records offline. It offers an MCP integration so Claude or ChatGPT can "search your notes and draft follow-ups" — [Blinq AI Notetaker](https://blinq.me/solutions/ai-notetaker)
  - Linking a note to a contact does not share it with that person — [Blinq support](https://support.blinq.me/en/articles/71264-ai-notetaker-the-basics)
  - Search covers all notes, including phone-call notes — [Blinq support](https://support.blinq.me/en/articles/76755-searching-your-ai-notes)
- **Blinq location**: The iOS privacy label lists precise location as collected — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blinq-digital-business-card/id1324102258). A Trustpilot reviewer (Sep 2026) praised notes about connections "and where meetings occurred" — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/blinq.me). The pages I fetched do not describe a map view or automatic geotag.
- **CamCard**: Real-time voice-to-text with AI summaries, a 120-minute free voice trial, and "AI Business Card Insights" (company profiles and financials) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id349447615). The privacy label lists precise and coarse location (not linked to identity) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id349447615). CamCard Business has meeting, visit and call notes, tags and smart categorisation, plus AI email writing — [CamCard pricing](https://www.camcard.com/pricing)
- **Popl**: "Voice recording for in-person meetings", intelligent note-taking, AI-generated follow-up emails, and "location/mapping features for event navigation" — [popl.co](https://popl.co/)
- **Covve**: "Voice Capture: create leads and notes via voice input"; an AI research assistant that enriches leads and personalises follow-ups — [covve.com](https://covve.com/). Notes, groups and "instant bios" are included in Essential — [Covve pricing](https://covve.com/pricing)
- **Wave Connect**: Tags, notes and context added while scanning stay "linked to the prospect" — [Wave docs](https://wavecnct.com/docs/ar/contacts/scanner.md). Teams can auto-tag event leads — [Wave pricing](https://wavecnct.com/pages/pricing)
- **HiHello**: "Tag contacts by event, role, or priority" and "add notes while the conversation's still fresh" — [HiHello scanner page](https://www.hihello.com/features/business-card-scanner). Auto-tagging is on Business and Enterprise — [HiHello pricing](https://www.hihello.com/pricing)
- **V1CE**: Event tagging, AI-suggested messages and the Scout assistant (researches contacts and suggests conversation openers) — [V1CE pricing](https://v1ce.co/pages/pricing)
- **CardDrop**: Event tagging for networking scenarios, plus notes — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10047916/carddrop/)
- **NeverDrop**, **AI CardVault** and **Leadnics** all offer voice notes. NeverDrop and Leadnics add AI follow-up emails or voice-driven tasks — [NeverDrop](https://dolphinvoice.ai/zh/ai-apps/never-drop-1080760); [AI CardVault](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ai-cardvault/id6740126760); [Leadnics](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6739613530)
- A generic Arabic scanner app lets users attach "locations" to scanned contacts — [App Store (ar)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%EC%8A%A4%EB%A7%88%ED%8A%B8-%EB%AA%85%ED%95%A8-%EB%A6%AC%EB%8D%94%EA%B8%B0/id6752925652?l=ar)

### Inferences
- **A map of where you met people, with a city picker, appears to be unclaimed ground** among card apps. Incumbents organise by event or tag, not by place or city. This suits someone moving between the UAE, Australia and Thailand.
- Blinq is the strongest rival on voice notes, with 99 languages, offline capture and private notes. Its framing is meetings and sales follow-up, not one quick tap to remember a person.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether any app automatically records GPS or date at capture, or shows a map. Privacy labels show location is collected, but not how it is used.
- I could not confirm whether any app captures a photo of the person, separate from the card or profile photo.
- I could not confirm whether Blinq's or Covve's voice notes are parsed into structured contact fields, as opposed to a transcript and summary.

## 5. Follow-up and data features: reminders, enrichment, AI search, WhatsApp, contacts sync and vCard, CRM, export, account deletion, offline

### Takeaway
CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, plus Zapier) and CSV/Excel export are standard on paid tiers. Enrichment is everywhere: LinkedIn, title and email lookup. Reminders and automated follow-ups exist in Wave, V1CE, CardDrop and Blinq. AI natural-language search is rare; only CardDrop and Blinq's note search and MCP integration are confirmed. **No product offered WhatsApp as a way to bring contacts or notes in**. WhatsApp appears only as a way to share your card out (CamCard). Offline capture is confirmed for Covve, Blinq (audio) and Wave.

### Cited Findings
- **Reminders / follow-ups**:
  - Wave: notes, tags and reminders, plus automated email/SMS follow-ups — [wavecnct.com](https://wavecnct.com/)
  - V1CE: automated follow-up sequences — [V1CE](https://v1ce.co/pages/pricing)
  - CardDrop: follow-up reminders on Pro — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10047916/carddrop/)
  - Blinq: "contact annotations and follow-ups" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/blinq-digital-business-card/id1324102258)
  - CamCard Business: task assignment — [CamCard pricing](https://www.camcard.com/pricing)
- **Enrichment**:

## 6. Privacy: human transcription of cards, data hosting, AI training, GDPR and certifications

- In the UAE, a capture tool with Arabic support (including bilingual Arabic/English cards) and WhatsApp intake faces little direct competition. The local players mainly sell NFC cards.
