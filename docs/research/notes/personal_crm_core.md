# Core personal CRM competitors (Mesh/Clay, Dex, folk, Covve, Monica): profiles as of October 2026

All official pages below were checked on 2026-10-07 unless stated otherwise. Where a fact comes from a third party or a competitor's own blog, it is labelled. Reddit pages could not be fetched (the domain is blocked for the research tool), so Reddit sentiment comes only from second-hand summaries and is flagged.

## 1. Status in 2026: alive, rebranded, acquired, pivoted or shut down? Notable 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
All five products are still running. Clay was bought by Automattic in June 2025 and renamed Mesh (me.sh) on 2026-03-20, and it reached Android in August 2026. Dex is alive and has moved toward AI and messaging: you can text it on WhatsApp or SMS. folk is still a team CRM, but it shipped its first mobile app ("Contacts by folk") in July 2026, followed by card scanning and talk-to-text. Covve's company now puts business-card and lead capture for teams first, and the personal CRM app gets little promotion. Monica is alive but in transition: the current stable code is from 2024, and a ground-up "Monica v3" rebuild is promised "before the end of 2026".

### Inferences
- Mesh is now a corporate side bet inside Automattic, with Beeper integration as its messaging angle. Its momentum (Android, Nexus AI, rebrand) is real, but its focus is network intelligence from inbox, calendar and social sources, not capture in the field.
- Dex is the competitor most clearly moving toward this app's concept: send a message by text or WhatsApp, including voice, and AI turns it into a contact.
- folk has only recently gone mobile (July-August 2026). Its mobile capture is new and aimed at teams doing sales.
- Covve looks like it has pivoted its marketing to B2B lead capture, and the personal CRM app may be in maintenance mode. Treat it as a weakened consumer competitor.
- Monica's "v3" naming clashes with GitHub's 4.x and 5.x versions. The 2023-2025 "Chandler" beta appears to be superseded by the 2026 rebuild, but this was not confirmed.

### Gaps
- Dex funding history and team size were not researched.
- The real release date of Covve's latest personal-app update could not be verified.
- Whether Monica's 2026 "v3" replaces the Chandler v5 beta, and how existing hosted users will migrate, is not stated in the sources fetched.
- I found no primary Automattic press release; the acquisition facts come from Crunchbase News, TechCrunch and AlternativeTo.

## 2. What each product does, who it targets, and platforms

### Takeaway
Mesh and Dex are personal or prosumer "network CRMs" that pull contacts in automatically from email, calendar and social accounts. folk is a lightweight sales and team CRM. Covve's personal app is a mobile-only contact book with reminders and news. Monica is a privacy-first, open-source "personal relationship manager" for family and friends as well as work contacts. Only Covve is mobile-only, and only Monica has no native mobile app today.

### Cited Findings
- **Mesh:** a "personal AI-friendly CRM that brings all your connections together". — [Mesh Library](https://library.me.sh/2026/03/20/mesh/)
  - Platforms: macOS, Windows, Web, Android, iOS. — [me.sh](https://me.sh/)
  - The iOS listing also mentions widgets and Siri Shortcuts. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mesh-contacts-crm/id1463073824)
  - A Team plan lets teams "see who knows whom". — [me.sh](https://me.sh/)
- **Dex:** a personal CRM that reminds you to keep in touch.
  - Platforms: web app, iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Chrome extension, an MCP server and a REST API. — [getdex.com](https://getdex.com/)
  - Typical users are in investing, business development, sales, PR, coaching and recruiting. — [Capterra/Dex listing summary](https://www.capterra.com/p/275020/Dex/reviews/)
- **folk:** a CRM for small teams, per-member pricing with unlimited members, including pipelines, email campaigns and sequences. — [folk pricing](https://www.folk.app/pricing)
  - The "Contacts by folk" mobile app runs on iOS and Android. — [folk mobile](https://www.folk.app/mobile)
  - Its G2 reviews skew toward small businesses and solo entrepreneurs. — [G2 folk reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/folk-folk/reviews)
- **Covve (personal CRM):** a mobile contact app on iOS and Android, with no desktop app. — [Covve Review (Dex blog, competitor)](https://getdex.com/blog/covve-review/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id958935377)
  - The separate Covve Scan / lead-capture product targets event and sales teams. — [Covve pricing](https://covve.com/pricing)
- **Monica:** an open-source web application, a personal relationship management system "for family, friends and business contacts". — [GitHub](https://github.com/monicahq/monica)
  - Native iOS and Android apps are "planned" for v3. — [Monica v3](https://www.monicahq.com/en/v3/)

### Inferences
- None of the five is positioned as "quiet capture of people you meet in person" for a traveller who meets people across several cities. Their mental models are "inbox-derived network" (Mesh, Dex), "team pipeline" (folk), "card scanner" (Covve) or "private journal or address book" (Monica).

### Gaps
- Current user counts were not found for Dex, folk, Covve or Monica hosted.

## 3. Capture speed for a person met in real life (voice, transcription, LLM extraction, photos, card OCR, geotag, map)

### Takeaway
Dex is closest to this app's concept. You can send Dex a WhatsApp or SMS message, including a voice message or photo, and it creates or updates the contact and parses reminders; its card scanner works offline and saves the card image. folk added AI card scanning and talk-to-text notes in July-August 2026. Mesh has card scanning and photo-based contact creation, plus an experimental hands-free voice mode on Android. Covve's strength is fast card OCR. Monica has none of these. **No product was verified to stamp the GPS location and date of a meeting automatically, or to show "where I met them" on a map.** Dex's map uses each contact's address or LinkedIn location, and only on web and desktop.

### Cited Findings

**Dex**
- You can text Dex by SMS or WhatsApp with plain text, voice messages, photos, documents or emojis, in natural language with no special commands. — [Dex docs: Messaging Dex](https://getdex.com/docs/ai/messaging-dex)
- A message like "Just met Sarah Chen at Starbucks…" makes Dex create or update the contact, extracting name, company, role and context. — [Dex docs: Messaging Dex](https://getdex.com/docs/ai/messaging-dex)
- Reminder requests are parsed, for example "Remind me to call Mike … next Tuesday". There is a morning digest (birthdays, people to reconnect with, today's meetings) and an end-of-day recap that asks about new connections. — [Dex docs: Messaging Dex](https://getdex.com/docs/ai/messaging-dex)
- Speech-to-text runs on Deepgram, and generation and summarization on OpenAI. — [Dex privacy policy](https://getdex.com/privacy)
- The card scanner is on iOS and Android. It auto-reads the card and pre-fills an editable form, and a picture of the card is saved to the contact. Near-instant results, sequential scanning at events, and it works offline (syncs on reconnect). — [Dex docs: card scanner](https://getdex.com/docs/workflows/card-scanner)
- The App Store description lists business-card scanning with voice-to-text note dictation, and a map/location view. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1472132715)
- The pricing page lists "voice mode" as a Professional-tier feature. — [Dex pricing](https://getdex.com/pricing)
- The map view plots contacts by their stated location or address, filled from LinkedIn location values, and is only on web/desktop. Filters exist for "Location" (text) and "Map Location" (distance from a point). — [Dex Guide](https://getdex.com/docs/workflows/organize-your-contacts) / [Dex Guide](https://getdex.com/docs/workflows/manage-your-contacts) (from search-result summaries of Dex docs)

**folk**
- The Contacts by folk mobile app (2026-07-16) lets you "Add new contacts in one-click from anywhere (including business card scanner)" and record notes "with voice-to-text". — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog/introducing-contacts-by-folk-our-mobile-app)
- AI business-card scanner (2026-07-31): "AI captures the details faster and more accurately than any existing scanners". — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog/scan-business-cards)
- Talk-to-text in the mobile app (2026-08-25). — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog)
- Contacts can be synced "from your phone, from your favorite platforms like WhatsApp or LinkedIn, and even from business cards". — [folk mobile](https://www.folk.app/mobile)
- The Standard plan lists "business card scanning, voice notes". — [folk pricing](https://www.folk.app/pricing)
- No location, map or offline features are mentioned. — [folk mobile](https://www.folk.app/mobile)

**Mesh**
- The homepage lists business-card scanning and "photo-based contact creation". — [me.sh](https://me.sh/)
- The iOS listing says business cards are digitized "with improved reliability". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mesh-contacts-crm/id1463073824)
- A voice-based, hands-free experience is experimental, and an improved card scanner is in development (Aug 2026). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/mesh-automattics-crm-for-everyone-comes-to-android)
- Contacts are mainly captured automatically from email, calendar, address book, LinkedIn, X and iMessage. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mesh-contacts-crm/id1463073824)
- Location is stored as City/State/Country/Location fields. — [Mesh export / Notion KB (search summary)](https://library.me.sh/knowledge-base/export-to-csv/)
- Nexus can answer location questions such as who to connect with when visiting New York. — [TechCrunch 2025](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3017988) (search summary)
- No map view was verified.

**Covve**
- The personal app offers card scanning with "fast, accurate OCR", smart follow-up reminders, notes and offline access. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id958935377)
- The Covve Scan / lead-capture product offers voice capture: "Create new leads and notes using your voice", plus unlimited scanning of cards, event badges and LinkedIn QR codes, offline scanning and 60+ languages. This is the team product, not the personal app. — [covve.com](https://covve.com/); [Covve pricing](https://covve.com/pricing)

**Monica**
- Monica's features are contact management, birthday/event reminders, activities, a journal, photo and document uploads, addresses and custom fields. It has no voice capture, card scanning or AI. — [GitHub](https://github.com/monicahq/monica)
- The v3 page does not mention AI, voice, photos or maps. — [Monica v3](https://www.monicahq.com/en/v3/)

### Inferences
- In practice, Dex's "text Dex" over WhatsApp is the closest analogue to this app's one-button voice note. But it lives inside a messaging thread, and does not stamp the GPS location of the meeting, offer a map of meeting places, or keep a photo of the place.
- A GPS-and-date stamp, a map of where people were met, and a city picker together look like open space among these five, based on what was verifiable.
- Card scanning is commodity: four of the five have it.

### Gaps
- I could not find out exactly what Dex's "voice mode" does on Professional, beyond voice messages to the Dex chatbot and dictation.
- I did not test end-to-end capture time (seconds from tap to saved contact) for any app. No vendor publishes this.
- Whether Mesh's or folk's card scan saves the card image was not verified (Dex's does).
- Whether any product keeps photos of the person or the place (beyond profile avatars, or Monica's general photo uploads) was not verified.

## 4. Reminders, enrichment, AI search, messaging integrations, contacts sync, export, offline, deletion, teams

### Takeaway
Mesh and Dex lead on keep-in-touch reminders, birthdays, enrichment and AI questions over your network. Dex is the only one with a WhatsApp/SMS chatbot for capture and questions. Monica leads on open formats (vCard, CardDAV) and has no enrichment by design. folk's reminders work through AI Assistants and Tasks. Export is CSV for Mesh and Dex, SQL for Monica hosted plus vCard per contact, and Excel or contacts export for Covve Scan. All except Monica sell team or multi-seat plans.

### Cited Findings

**Mesh**
- All plans include reconnect reminders, birthday notifications, and "life updates" (job and location changes, news mentions), plus imports from email, calendar, X, LinkedIn, Facebook and iMessage. — [Mesh pricing](https://me.sh/pricing)
- Nexus is an "AI navigator for your network", alongside natural-language search. — [me.sh](https://me.sh/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mesh-contacts-crm/id1463073824)
- Integrations: Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Notion, WhatsApp, X, email, iMessage, Beeper. — [me.sh](https://me.sh/)
- Also listed on iOS: Gmail, Exchange, Office365, iCloud, Airtable, Pipedrive, Close. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mesh-contacts-crm/id1463073824)
- Export is "Export my Data", which emails a CSV compatible with Google Contacts. Some licensed fields are excluded from the export. — [Mesh KB: Export to CSV](https://library.me.sh/knowledge-base/export-to-csv/) (search summary)
- Accounts can be deleted in-app or by emailing care@me.sh. Backups are kept up to 90 days. — [Mesh security & privacy](https://library.me.sh/knowledge-base/security-and-privacy/)
- Teams: the Team plan covers up to 4 members; Enterprise has unlimited members. — [Mesh pricing](https://me.sh/pricing)

**Dex**
- Dex pulls from 15+ sources: email, calendar, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, iMessage, Facebook, Instagram, phone contacts and CSV. It merges duplicates and offers keep-in-touch reminders and a timeline of interactions. Network updates flag role changes. — [getdex.com](https://getdex.com/)
- Premium includes two-way contact sync, LinkedIn sync (up to 2,500 connections; 9,000 on Professional), WhatsApp and messaging sync, and 10 research credits a month. — [Dex pricing](https://getdex.com/pricing)
- WhatsApp sync runs daily after you link by QR code. It imports contacts from individual chats and small groups and shows the latest message on each profile. — [Dex WhatsApp](https://getdex.com/product/whatsapp/)
- AI search works by text, for example "Who did I meet at the Google event who works in product management?". — [Dex docs](https://getdex.com/docs/ai/messaging-dex)
- Pre-meeting briefs. — [getdex.com](https://getdex.com/)
- Export is CSV only, from Settings → Workflow → Import/Export. Fields include birthday, phone, keep-in-touch frequency, groups and custom fields. No vCard export was found. — [Dex docs: export](https://getdex.com/docs/workflows/export) (search summary)
- Account deletion is in settings or by emailing contact@getdex.com, and is irreversible. — [Dex privacy](https://getdex.com/privacy)
- The Team plan is $40/user. — [Dex pricing](https://getdex.com/pricing)

**folk**
- AI Assistants: Follow-up and Recap. — [Capsule CRM (competitor)](https://capsulecrm.com/blog/folk-crm-alternatives/); [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog)
- Plan features: enrichment credits (500/month on Standard), Magic fields, a LinkedIn extension, and email, calendar and WhatsApp sync. — [folk pricing](https://www.folk.app/pricing)
- Phone-number enrichment arrived 2026-10-01. — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog)
- In the mobile app you can "ask folk a question, and we'll find the right contact". — [folk mobile](https://www.folk.app/mobile)
- folk MCP is available. — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog)
- folk claims "Data export and deletion tools", without details. — [folk GDPR article, updated 2026-07-22](https://www.folk.app/articles/gdpr-compliant-crm-hosted-in-europe)
- All plans include unlimited members. — [folk pricing](https://www.folk.app/pricing)

**Covve (personal app)**
- Smart follow-up reminders, career news on contacts (scanned from 150+ news sources), weekly and monthly networking stats, tags, a "24/7 AI assistant via email", sync across devices, and offline access. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id958935377); [Covve App Store summary](https://apps.apple.com/app/id958935377)
- Covve Scan exports to phone contacts or Excel, and integrates with Outlook, Google Contacts and Zapier; Pro adds CRM add-ons. — [Covve pricing](https://covve.com/pricing)
- Account deletion for the personal app was not found.

**Monica**
- Features: birthday and event reminders, activities, tasks, a journal, multiple vaults and user accounts. — [GitHub](https://github.com/monicahq/monica)
- vCard import has existed since v0.2.0, export of single contacts as vCard is supported, and Monica can act as a CardDAV server. — [Monica blog v0.2.0](https://www.monicahq.com/blog/v0.2.0); search summary of Monica docs and forks
- Hosted export is SQL, available in-app, via API or on request. — [Monica privacy](https://www.monicahq.com/en/privacy)
- v3 promises full export including custom structures. — [Monica v3](https://www.monicahq.com/en/v3/)

- Dex's daily WhatsApp sync and its text-the-bot channel are both already built. This sets the bar for this app's planned WhatsApp voice-note intake.
- Only Monica clearly supports vCard and CardDAV. The others export CSV, so vCard export plus contacts sync would differentiate this app from Mesh, Dex and folk.

### Gaps
- Offline capture was not verified for Mesh, folk or Monica. Dex is verified offline only for card scanning.
- Whether phone-contacts sync is two-way was verified only for Dex ("two-way contact sync"). Mesh and folk say "sync" without saying which direction.
- Account deletion for folk and Covve was not verified on primary pages.

## 5. Privacy stance and data practices

### Takeaway
Monica is the most privacy-pure: open source, no AI, no ads, no data sales. But the hosted database is not encrypted at rest beyond passwords. Covve's personal app offers true end-to-end encryption with a 9-word key that cannot be recovered. Mesh is SOC 2 Type II and says it does not train models on user data. Dex runs on US Google Cloud, and enriches through third-party public-data vendors (EnrichLayer, Bright Data) and LLMs (OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, OpenRouter). folk hosts data in the EU and sells enrichment credits. Dex, Mesh and folk all enrich contacts with third-party data.

### Cited Findings
- **Mesh:** SOC 2 Type II, data encrypted in transit and at rest, AI features opt-in, and "Nexus and Mesh do not use your data to train our models". Third-party AI partners are not named, and hosting location is not given. — [Mesh security & privacy](https://library.me.sh/knowledge-base/security-and-privacy/)
  - A HIPAA BAA is available. — [Mesh pricing](https://me.sh/pricing)
  - The iOS privacy label lists contact info, contacts, emails/messages and search history as linked to you, used for app functionality, analytics and marketing. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mesh-contacts-crm/id1463073824)
- **Dex:** servers and databases are in the US, on Google Cloud Platform and Firebase. Dex uses "SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified providers"; this is the providers' certification, not Dex's own. Encryption is AES-256 at rest and TLS in transit, and Dex says it "never sell[s] your data". — [Dex security](https://getdex.com/security); [Dex privacy](https://getdex.com/privacy)
  - AI and enrichment vendors: OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, OpenRouter, EnrichLayer (public professional profiles) and Bright Data (infrastructure to retrieve public data). Dex does not use Google user data to train models. For data sent to AI providers via MCP, Dex disclaims responsibility for those providers' training. — [Dex privacy, updated 2026-09-23](https://getdex.com/privacy)
- **folk:** "Built in France", "Customer data is hosted in Europe", and a "GDPR-first approach". Certifications and the AI training stance are not stated on that page. — [folk GDPR article](https://www.folk.app/articles/gdpr-compliant-crm-hosted-in-europe)
  - European data-hosting migration is dated 2026-09-24. — [folk changelog](https://www.folk.app/changelog)
  - folk sells enrichment and phone-number enrichment credits. — [folk pricing](https://www.folk.app/pricing)
- **Covve:** end-to-end encryption covers notes, interactions, reminders and family info. The key is a 9-word phrase stored only on the device; if you lose it, you cannot get past login and the data cannot be recovered. — [Covve Help, updated 2025-10-30](https://help.covve.com/help/introducing-end-to-end-encryption-kb)
  - Covve is ISO 27001 certified, GDPR compliant and CyberVadis Platinum. — [Covve search summary of covve.com](https://covve.com/)
  - The iOS privacy label says some data is used for the developer's advertising and marketing. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id958935377)
- **Monica:** hosted on Fortrabbit servers, location not stated. Data "is not encrypted in the database" (passwords are bcrypt-hashed), there is optional 2FA, no analytics trackers, and Monica will not sell data. — [Monica privacy](https://www.monicahq.com/en/privacy)
  - "No advertising. No sale of personal data. No model trained on your contacts." — [Monica v3](https://www.monicahq.com/en/v3/)

### Inferences
- A privacy-forward message such as "your notes, no enrichment, no scraping, no training" would clearly separate this app from Dex, folk and Mesh, which all use third-party enrichment. Covve shows that end-to-end encryption is a real selling point in this category, but it costs recoverability.
- Where data is hosted matters for UAE, Australian and Thai users. Only folk (EU) and Dex (US) state a region, and Mesh, Covve and Monica do not.

### Gaps
- The AI providers Mesh uses and its hosting region were not found.
- Whether folk holds SOC 2 or ISO certification, and its AI-training stance, were not verified.
- The privacy policy for Covve's personal app beyond end-to-end encryption was not fetched.

## 7. Notable user complaints and praise

### Takeaway
Reviewers praise Mesh's design and steady development. They complain about bugs (missing notes, Nexus not working, failed iMessage imports, duplicates) at a high annual price. Dex is praised for import and cadence features but criticized for lag, sync problems and a broken map view. folk is loved for its UX, LinkedIn and WhatsApp links and support, but G2 reviewers long complained it had no mobile app (fixed July 2026). Covve is praised for privacy and design, with complaints about sync and duplication, login trouble and price. Monica dominates self-hosted discussions.

### Cited Findings

  - Praise (Sept 2024): the reviewer was happy to pay the annual renewal because development and support were consistent.
  - Complaint (Jan 22, likely 2026): missing contact notes, Nexus not working, contacts could not be deleted. The reviewer wanted a refund on the $120 annual plan.
  - Complaint (Mar 2024): "Clay is super buggy and unfocused on its core promise", including failed iMessage imports and no automatic duplicate resolution.

  - Praise (Oct 2021): imports and cadence features better than Cloze or HubSpot.
  - Complaints: lag and sync issues leading to cancellation (Nov 2023); a broken LinkedIn sync, faulty import checkboxes and a "non-functional map view" (Nov 2024).
  - Capterra: 5.0 from only 3 reviews. Users praise its simplicity and ask for macOS Mail and Apple Messages integration. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/275020/Dex/reviews/)
  - Product Hunt: 5.0 from 5 reviews. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/dex)

  - Praise: responsive support, LinkedIn integration, an intuitive UI, and email and WhatsApp channels.
  - Complaints: no mobile app (predates the July 2026 launch), email sync problems, and limited pipeline, forecasting and automation features.

  - Praise: a reviewer called it "critical component of my daily routine" (Oct 2021).
